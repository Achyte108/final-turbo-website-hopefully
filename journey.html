Our Journey

September 2023

The formation of Turbo

The administration at ISC-DIP greenlit the formation of the school's first ever electric car team. This team became known as ISC-DIP Turbo led by 2 students of the class of 2025

March 2024

First ever EVGP

In 2024, team turbo was given the privilege to partake in the 2024 edition of the Electric Vehicle Grand Prix winning several awards

September 2024

Team Turbo began its second year of operation

EVGP

February 2025

Under the leaders of 2024 team turbo was led to its second year in a row at evgp where many awards were once again won
